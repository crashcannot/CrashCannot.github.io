CrashCannot

High-performance exploit prevention built to sanitize packet payloads and prevent server overloading.

📦 Item Data Optimization

Identifies, intercepts, and safely drops bloated structural data payloads or NBT-heavy items before they cross the network thread to freeze chunk loaders.

⚡ Packet Rate Limiting

Silently drops automated crash script frequencies and abnormal macro inject patterns without increasing network latency or interrupting clean player connections.

👤 About the Developer

Hi, I'm Derrik Sanchez.

I have been developing software for over 6 years. Like many of you reading this, I've spent plenty of time running and hosting Minecraft servers. I know exactly what it feels like to have your community interrupted or crashed repeatedly by exploiters trying to find loops, and I absolutely hate it.

Instead of just taking the problem, I decided to fix it. For the past 3 years, I have engineered and refined CrashCannot to provide clean, definitive fixes against network overloads.

We are here to help you out the best we can and we will provide active support in our GitHub repository tracker.
